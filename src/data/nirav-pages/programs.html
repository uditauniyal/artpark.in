About ARTPARK

Our programs.

ARTPARK’s programs span three tracks: ArtLabs runs mission-led work in robotics, Industry 5.0 and connectivity, along with the Academy that builds the talent to deploy it; Venture Building backs researchers and startups taking deep tech from lab to market; and Social Impact puts health and language data and AI to work for the public.

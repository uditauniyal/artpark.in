Health Data & AI

Data and AI-driven public health responses to endemic, epidemic and climate-related threats. Leading computational epidemiology groups work with city, state and national governments, in an open-innovation ecosystem.

What it is building

01

PRISM-H · dengue

A live system in Karnataka that combines epidemiological trends, weather and environmental data to forecast dengue outbreaks at sub-district level, up to four weeks ahead. Recognised in the UN World Cities Report 2024; SKOCH Award for E-Governance, 2025.

02

Heat-risk forecasting

Sub-district heat-risk predictions up to seven days ahead, built with the Government of Karnataka (KSNDMC, KSDMA and the Department of Health). Winner of Grand Challenges India and a winner-finalist of the IndiaAI Innovation Challenge 2025.

03

Dharini · One Health

An open-source surveillance platform that brings together clinical, environmental and genomic surveillance of pathogens, focused on avian influenza, lumpy skin disease and foot-and-mouth disease.

04

Livestock vaccination strategy

Supporting the Empowered Committee for Animal Health with evidence for disease-control strategy, starting with foot-and-mouth disease, which ICAR estimates costs ₹20,000 crore a year.

05

High-risk pregnancy copilot

A WhatsApp assistant in Hindi, Telugu and Marathi that gives ANMs expert-verified guidance on 35 high-risk conditions. Deployed with 3,000+ ANMs in Uttar Pradesh, Telangana and Maharashtra. Gates Foundation Global Grand Challenges winner (2023); Google.org accelerator (2025).

06

Kilkari AI chatbot

Trusted answers about pregnancy and infant care on WhatsApp, piloted with 1,505 mothers in Mumbai’s mMitra programme and designed for Kilkari, which reaches 3M+ women. Winner of Grand Challenges India by BIRAC.

07

AI trainer for community mental health

A multi-agent trainer, built with CMHLP, to help train ASHAs in community mental health at scale. In prototyping.

08

MIDAS · screening

India-specific medical imaging datasets, with ICMR and IISc. They power the Aarogya Aarohan oral cancer screening app (40k+ patients screened, 8 trial sites) and AI chest X-ray triage for TB, compatible with NIKSHAY.

09

Calibrate

An open-source platform for evaluating AI agents. Raising The Village and The Agency Fund used it to test a field-agent tool for farming families in rural Uganda, catching nearly 150 errors before launch.

Language Data & AI

India has 22 scheduled languages, hundreds of dialects and over a billion speakers. Most AI understands none of them well. ARTPARK, with IISc, is building the data foundation to change that.

In numbers

31.2k

hours of speech data

109

165

156k+

speakers recorded

What it is building

01

Vaani

India’s largest open-source speech dataset, by IISc and ARTPARK and funded by Google: real voices from 165 districts, open via Bhashini and Hugging Face. The goal is 150,000+ hours from every district in India.

02

RESPIN

Speech recognition datasets in 9 Indian languages, focused on agriculture and finance for the poor. A digital public good, open to all.

03

SYSPIN

Open text-to-speech corpora and synthesis models in 9 Indian languages, for voice interfaces in health, education and governance.

04

SraVaani 1.0

An open speech-recognition model for 65 Indian languages and dialects, built on Vaani data.

The trade-offs

An honest look at the trade-offs.

Pros

+Direct investment — sized to your round, not a fixed grant.
+You retain your IP and control of the company.
+Access to ARTPARK’s customer, government and corporate pipeline.
+A faster path to revenue and scale for already-demonstrated technology.

Cons

−A higher bar — you need a registered entity, team, pitch and TRL 4+ technology.
−Investment is dilutive from day one; terms are negotiated, not standard.
−Less hand-holding than TIR — ARTPARK acts as investor and enabler, not residency host.
FAQ

Answers to the questions we get most.

Do I need to be incorporated?+

Yes. VIP is for incorporated Indian startups — typically Private Limited companies. DPIIT recognition is preferred but not required. If you’re pre-incorporation, TIR is the right door.

What stage does VIP expect?+

A working team, a pitch investors can engage with, and technology at TRL 4 or above. Early revenue or customer LOIs strengthen an application but are not hard requirements.

How is funding structured?+

A direct equity investment, not a fixed grant. Ticket size is sized to your round, with terms negotiated per deal and shared with shortlisted teams.

Do I need to be on campus?+

Barring selective cases where remote makes sense, most VIP startups are encouraged to work full-time from ARTPARK’s Garage facility.

Can a TIR alumnus apply?+

Yes — it’s a common pathway. TIR teams who incorporate and reach TRL 4+ can pitch to future Investment Committees.

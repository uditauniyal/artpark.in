What is RDI

Long-tenure capital for research that's ready to scale.

The Research, Development and Innovation (RDI) Scheme, anchored by the Department of Science & Technology through the Anusandhan National Research Foundation, provides patient, low-cost capital to private-sector R&D in sunrise and strategic sectors — for technologies at Technology Readiness Level 4 and above.

01

Scale private R&D

Encourage the private sector to invest in research and innovation in strategic domains.

02

Fund the hard middle

Finance transformative projects at higher readiness levels, where capital is scarcest.

03

Acquire critical tech

Support acquisition of technologies of strategic importance to India.

What it can invest in

Instruments.

Long-term loansLow or nil interest, long tenure — sized for development cycles commercial lenders won't carry.
EquityDirect investment in startups taking research to market.
Fund of FundsContributions to deep-tech funds that invest further into innovation.

Eligible: Indian startups and companies with TRL 4+ technology in strategic or sunrise sectors. Through ARTPARK, the focus is AI, robotics and autonomous systems for aerospace & defence, space tech, medtech and advanced manufacturing.

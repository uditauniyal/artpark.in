Advanced Robotic Systems

Driving next-gen robotics in India through four focused innovation initiatives. Each targets a critical gap in robotics capability, with the aim of indigenous, intelligent and resilient systems.

In numbers

2,800

What it is building

01

Humanoids

Agile, dexterous humanoid robots for factory automation and healthcare assistance, integrating perception, manipulation, locomotion and foundation AI models for human-safe interaction. Built with industry partners to move quickly from prototype to deployment.

02

Resilient multi-robot systems

Collaborative networks of aerial, ground and underwater robots for defence and civilian use, with distributed AI, multimodal sensing and autonomous coordination for surveillance and disaster management.

03

Secure navigation stack (S-Nav)

Modular, encrypted navigation for autonomous systems that integrates GNSS, LiDAR and IMU sensors and is designed to meet ISO standards. ARTPARK startups such as Yaanendriya contribute to this ecosystem.

04

Physical AI

Robot data farms, simulation centres, robot foundation models and human-guided training, so robots can learn faster and transfer skills across tasks.

ArtLabs program

Integrated Industry 5.0 Platform

Purpose-driven, people-centric technology for Indian industries. AI and automation for India’s 1.6 million MSMEs, designed for how Indian manufacturing actually works.

What it is building

01

AI for the economy

Indian MSMEs produce 45% of industrial output and 40% of exports, yet fragmented data, reactive decision-making and manual oversight cost the sector time, yield and competitiveness. The platform embeds AI directly into industrial workflows.

02

The connectivity layer

5G private networks for mobile robots and drones, deterministic Ethernet TSN for safety-critical synchronisation, and Wi-Fi 7 for dense sensor networks.

03

The physical workforce

From caged industrial robots to cobots working safely beside people, drones for inspection, and mobile manipulators.

04

Envisaged impact

Automation of compliance and shop-floor operations, end-to-end traceability of assets, a lower carbon footprint with better sustainability reporting, and real-time decision making.

Partners in discussion: Bosch, Aequs, 7th Gen Ventures and KLE Tech.

Bharat 5G Labs (DoT)

What it is building

01

5G test lab

A Bharat 5G Use Case Lab and a parallel Karnataka 5G Test Lab, with private 5G coverage across ARTGarage indoors and outdoors. Open to startups, academia and industry at nominal cost.

02

5G-enabled drones and UGVs

Multi-UAV and UGV operations over 5G, including beyond-visual-line-of-sight flights and a reference architecture for drone corridor management.

03

InterSafe

A 5G road-safety system: roadside cameras, radar and sensors with edge AI that send hazard warnings to vehicles and phones in real time.

04

LifeCircuit

A 5G emergency-care network that activates nearby first responders, finds the nearest defibrillator and alerts ambulances and hospitals.

About ARTPARK · Introduction

Building India’s next generation of innovations and innovators.

ARTPARK (AI & Robotics Technology Park) at IISc, Bengaluru, is a nonprofit (section-8) organisation building India’s next generation of innovations and innovators for its economy, sovereignty and society.

01 · What we do

A deeptech venture builder for AI, robotics and autonomous systems.

It is a deeptech venture builder focused on AI, robotics and autonomous systems-related technologies. Sectors of interest include advanced manufacturing, aerospace & defence, spacetech, mobility, and medtech.

02 · How we began

Created by IISc, backed by government and industry.

Created by IISc under the National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems (NM-ICPS), Department of Science & Technology, Government of India, it has been supported as a centre of excellence by Dept of IT BT, Government of Karnataka and Ministry of Heavy Industries, in addition to leading companies and foundations.

NEW · RDI SCHEME

ARTPARK was recently chosen by DST as a fund manager for the RDI (Research, Development & Innovation) Scheme, which is set to transform the landscape of deep-tech investments in India.

03 · Certifications

Registrations and recognitions.

As a charitable organisation seed-funded by DST, ARTPARK is eligible for Section 80G deductions and CSR contributions.

CERTIFIED

Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation (SIRO)

Department of Scientific & Industrial Research (DSIR)

Recognition from DSIR, the nodal department for in-house R&D units under the Ministry of Science and Technology. It lets ARTPARK avail certain customs-duty exemptions on imports, and recognises its work at the boundary of research and real-world use.

CERTIFIED

Eligible for CSR activities

Ministry of Corporate Affairs · CSR00017521

ARTPARK is registered to undertake Corporate Social Responsibility activities. Companies required to contribute under section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013 can contribute to ARTPARK to meet their CSR obligation.

CERTIFIED

Eligible for Section 80G tax deduction

Income Tax Department · Section 80G

Contributions to ARTPARK are eligible for a 50% tax deduction under Section 80G(2)(a)(iv), subject to an overall limit of 10% of the taxpayer’s gross total income.

CERTIFIED

Registered as a charitable institution

Income Tax Department

ARTPARK is registered as a company engaged in charitable activities, so grants it receives are exempt from income tax, subject to the requirements of the tax law.

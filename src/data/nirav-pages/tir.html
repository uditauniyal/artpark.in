FAQ

Answers to the questions we get most.

Would I be paid a stipend?+

Yes — indirectly. The ₹25L initial grant is structured to cover reasonable founder stipends, prototyping, travel and direct project expenses. You decide the allocation; ARTPARK reviews it as part of the milestone plan.

What’s the minimum TRL to apply?+

Your core IP should be at TRL 3 — lab or in-house validated. During the residency you mature it to TRL 4, alongside identifying product-market fit and customers.

Can I apply as a team?+

Yes — teams of up to 3 founders are encouraged. If you’re affiliated with a university, lab or company, a No-Objection Certificate is required before you join.

What happens after TIR?+

High-performing teams progress into the Venture Incubation Programme, with follow-on equity investment for the spin-out. If you don’t get funded through this pathway, you can license the IP and raise externally.

What sectors are a fit?+

AI, robotics, novel materials, sensors, mechatronics, manufacturing and cyber-physical systems — applied to manufacturing, space, health, agriculture, defence and similar critical sectors. Pure SaaS or consumer apps are not a fit.
